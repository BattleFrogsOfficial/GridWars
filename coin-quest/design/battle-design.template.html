A design for Coin Quest's center tab. You stand in a pixel world facing one debt at a time; swipe left or right to face the next. Monsters are rendered from small 3D models at their true sizes, and they take more visible damage the more of the debt you pay off.

Center stage
The monster stands on the path ahead, in a backdrop that matches its type: Mossy Hollow, Goblin Woods, Stone Bridge, Ember Canyon, the Dragon's Lair or the Castle Gate. Every monster is drawn with the same camera, so they keep their real sizes: a slime is a small blob at your feet, a troll towers, and the elder dragon hovers in the air above you.
Type never changes
A debt's monster is set by the highest balance it has ever had. Paying it down doesn't shrink it into a weaker monster; it wears it down instead: slashes and wounds, chunks knocked out, torn wings, duller colours and a hurt lean, in five stages until it's defeated.
Swiping
Drag the scene, use the arrows or press ← →. The monster slides away and turns side-on as it goes, the scenery pans as if you turned your head, and the next one turns toward you as it arrives. Let go before a quarter of the width and it settles back.
Attacking
Pay offers two choices: Minimum payment (one tap, amount shown) or Custom amount (type it or pick a share, with the attack it makes shown live). Once it's saved, the attack plays: five tiers by the share of the full debt (its highest balance) the payment takes off, from a Slash to a Legendary Strike, and a finishing blow when the debt reaches zero.
Money bar
Instead of a health bar: green banknotes for what's still owed. As you pay, it shrinks and the paid part fills with gold coins; under it, how much is paid of the total. New wounds show on the monster as each fifth is paid. Boss marks at 75/50/25% sit on the bar.
What the art says
Type from the peak balance (Slime under $500 up to Elder Dragon over $40k; a mortgage is a Castle). Colour from the interest rate: calm under 5%, angry 5–15%, furious 15%+. Frames read as rank: wood, iron, bronze, silver, gold, obsidian.

Attacks

Paying a debt happens once or twice a month, so every payment is a staged moment. The tier comes from the share of the full debt (its highest balance) the payment takes off. Play any of them on the phone above, on whichever monster you're facing.

AttackShare of the debtLengthWhat happens
Slime: squashes flat and wobbles; goo flies, and big hits splat goo on your screen that drips off.
Goblin: knocked back a step, spinning; chips fly.
Troll: barely moves, just shudders; dust bursts off the ground.
Young Dragon: rears back; scales go flying.
Elder Dragon: blasted upward in the air; scales scatter.
Castle: rumbles and sinks; bricks tumble and bounce, dust rolls out.
Finishing blow: the monster flashes three times and dissolves into gold coins, then "Defeated!" and a rain of gold.
