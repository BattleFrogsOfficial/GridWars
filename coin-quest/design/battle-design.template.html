Battle screen

A design for Coin Quest's center tab. You stand in a pixel world facing one debt at a time; swipe left or right to face the next. Monsters are rendered from small 3D models, so they turn as they leave and enter.

Center stage
The monster stands on the path a few steps ahead, in a backdrop that matches its type: Mossy Hollow, Goblin Woods, Stone Bridge, Ember Canyon, the Dragon's Lair or the Castle Gate. Your shield and sword frame the bottom of the view.
Swiping
Drag the scene, use the arrows or press ← →. The current monster slides and turns away from you; the scenery pans as if you turned your head; the next monster turns to face you as it arrives. Let go before a quarter of the width and it settles back.
Attacking
Attack (pay) opens the payment form in the app. Here it plays the sword thrust, a white hit flash and the damage number, and the HP bar drops. Boss marks at 75/50/25% sit on the bar.
What the art says
Monster type comes from the balance (Slime under $500 up to Elder Dragon over $40k; a mortgage is a Castle). Colour comes from the interest rate: calm under 5%, angry 5–15%, furious 15%+. Frames read as rank: wood, iron, bronze, silver, gold, obsidian.
